PKR Devaluation Early-Warning System

Composite alert based on historical pattern of all major PKR devaluations (2008, 2013, 2018, 2022, 2023). Score 0-100 — higher = greater devaluation probability over next 3-6 months.

Trigger Component Scoring

How the Alert Works

The 7 historical devaluation triggers — each scored 0-100:
  1. FX Reserves Stress (weight 25%): Reserves below 2 months of imports = high alert. Historical devaluation episodes ALL had reserves below 2.5 months of import cover.
  2. Current Account Deterioration (weight 20%): Sustained monthly deficit OR widening trend triggers alert. 2018 and 2022 episodes both began with CAD widening 30%+ YoY.
  3. REER Overvaluation (weight 15%): REER >103 = building pressure; >106 = imminent correction risk. Sep 2018 (REER 106.6) preceded major fall. April 2021 peak 102.95 preceded 2022 collapse.
  4. Inflation Pressure (weight 15%): CPI >12% combined with above triggers historically forced SBP-IMF action. 2022-23 peaked at 38% CPI.
  5. Fiscal Deficit Stress (weight 10%): Fiscal deficit >6.5% historically coincides with currency pressure. 2018: 6.6%, 2022: 7%+.
  6. Oil Price Shock (weight 10%): Arab Light >$85 sustained = import bill blow-out. 2008 (oil $140), 2022 (oil $120+).
  7. Real Interest Rate (weight 5%): Negative real rates (SBP rate − CPI <0) signal monetary slippage. Was negative throughout 2022.

Common Pattern From History

Every major PKR devaluation event since 2008 had these 5 common factors present 3-6 months BEFORE the crash:
  • FX reserves declining toward 2 months of import cover (or below)
  • Current account in widening deficit (sustained for 3+ months)
  • REER above 100 (overvalued in real terms)
  • External debt service obligations rising near term
  • IMF program either expiring, suspended, or about to enter
If 4 of these 5 fire simultaneously, devaluation has historically occurred within 6 months 100% of the time (n=5 episodes: 2008, 2013, 2017-18, 2022, 2023).

Key Insights From Historical Pattern Analysis

1. Reserve Levels Below 2-Month Import Cover Are The Single Strongest Predictor: Every one of the 5 major devaluation events occurred when FX reserves fell below 2 months of import cover. Currently Pakistan reserves at ~2.4 months — close to but above the threshold.

2. REER Above 100 Is A Necessary But Not Sufficient Condition: REER overvaluation alone doesn't trigger devaluation, but EVERY devaluation episode had REER >100 prior. The current REER at 105.80 is in danger zone — only above 106 in Sep 2018 before that crash. Watch this closely.

3. Twin Deficit (Current + Fiscal >12% GDP combined) Is The Killer Combination: 2008 had CA -8.4% + Fiscal -7.6% = 16% twin. 2018 had CA -6.3% + Fiscal -6.6% = 12.9% twin. 2022 had CA -4.6% + Fiscal -7.9% = 12.5% twin. Current Pakistan: CA approx -1% + Fiscal -6% = ~7% twin — much better than devaluation history.

4. IMF Program Status Is Counter-Intuitive: Devaluations cluster AROUND IMF program entry (forced by IMF as precondition) OR AROUND IMF program suspension (lost discipline). Mid-program periods are stable. Currently Pakistan in mid-EFF program — stable phase.

5. The 6-12 Month Lead Time: Reserves erosion typically begins 6-12 months before the formal devaluation. By the time politicians admit there's a problem, the crash has already happened. The dashboard's value is catching this LEADING.

How the Classification Works

Two conditions determine the zone:

  • Condition A — Rate vs Mean: Is the latest SBP policy rate above or below the long-term mean of ~11.01%?
  • Condition B — Direction: Is the rate increasing (last change was a hike) or decreasing (last change was a cut)? Holds inherit the prior direction.

The four zones:

  • DARK GREEN (Weight 1.0): Rate Lesser than Mean AND Decreasing → Maximum stocks. Optimal accumulation environment.
  • LIGHT GREEN (Weight 0.8): Rate Greater than Mean AND Decreasing → Heavy stocks. Easing cycle priced in.
  • YELLOW (Weight 0.6): Rate Lesser than Mean AND Increasing → Moderate stocks. Early tightening warning.
  • RED (Weight 0.2): Rate Greater than Mean AND Increasing → Minimum stocks. Tightening cycle in progress.

Why we are in RED right now: After cutting to 10.50% in December 2025 (and holding through March 2026), SBP unexpectedly hiked to 11.50% on April 27, 2026 — the first hike since June 2023. The hike was triggered by April 2026 CPI spiking to 10.9% (from 7.3% in March), a flip back to current account deficit (–$324M), and REER pushing to a 7.5-year high of 105.80. This single change moves rate above Mean AND establishes Increasing direction = RED quadrant.

What triggers a zone change: Either (a) the next MPC meeting cuts (would shift back to LIGHT GREEN at first cut, then DARK GREEN if below Mean), or (b) the rate moves materially. The current 11.50% is only 49bp above Mean — close call. Any hold-and-cut sequence in June/July 2026 could shift this back to LIGHT GREEN quickly.

6. Multibagger Track vs Explosive Stocks Track — Methodological Comparison

Why the Explosive Stocks Track Differs From Pure Multibaggers

The Explosive Stocks track (Tab 11) is built for quality-growth compounders showing an earnings inflection. It applies two IM3 signals — Signal A (revenue and EPS growth each ≥15%, the quality-growth tag) and Signal B (EPS growth >20% with EPS/Rev growth ratio >1.5, the earnings-acceleration trigger), with cash-flow tests (CFONP, CFONP%, SCFOSNP) as guardrails rather than hard pass/fail gates. It would systematically pass over genuine early-stage multibaggers because:

  • Cash guardrails (CFO vs NP): A multibagger in capex expansion phase often has cash flow below net profit temporarily. PABC during plant expansion would have tripped these guardrails.
  • Quality-growth tag needs both engines: Multibaggers in transition years or recovering from cyclical troughs may not yet show both revenue AND EPS at ≥15% — but be on the cusp of a 5-year acceleration.
  • Inflection timing: Pure multibaggers in their earliest years often haven't reached the Signal B acceleration window yet. They typically show strong qualitative drivers (moat, sponsor, TAM) before the financials inflect.

The Multibagger track relaxes the financial gates AND adds 5 qualitative criteria (M7 sponsor holding, M9 TAM, M10 moat type, M11 reinvestment runway, M12 owner-operator) — so genuine multibaggers can pass while quality discipline is preserved through the qualitative gates.
