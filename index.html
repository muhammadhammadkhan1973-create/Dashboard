Fundamentals: still red for adding equity, with one mildly better sign. Interest rates are 12% and still rising. The State Bank hiked on 26 Oct, and the next decision is 14 Dec. Inflation may be leveling off. The finance ministry projects October CPI at 9.5–10.5%, against September’s 10.3%. If that holds into November and December, it’s the first hint that the rate peak is getting close. That’s what would trigger Leg 1, the move from money market to debt. Oil is a headwind. Brent is at about $116 after attacks on Saudi energy sites, and Pakistan imports its oil. Global risk-off: the tech sell-off hit Asia today (Korea −5%, Japan −3%) and spilled into Pakistani banks, oil and cement names. Foreign investors were net sellers earlier this week. Cash still pays more. Money market yields about 12%, so equity has to clearly beat that before switching. MCB pension verdict: HOLD 10% equity / 20% debt / 70% money market. Leg 1 (money market to debt): not yet. Wait for the State Bank to stop hiking. The October CPI print in early November is the next clue. Leg 2 (into equity): not yet. It needs a confirmed chart bottom and supportive fundamentals together. Monday’s 15:50 check will also include the monthly fundamental review: MCB’s October fund report, AKD and Business Recorder.
